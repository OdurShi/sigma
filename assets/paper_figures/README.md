# Figures of the paper

The numbered figures of "SIGMA: Label-Efficient Training of Language Models to Follow
Changing Structured Guidance", cropped from the manuscript PDF without their captions at
200 dpi. Regenerate them with
`python scripts/extract_paper_figures.py /path/to/paper.pdf` (requires PyMuPDF).

| File | Caption as printed in the paper |
|---|---|
| `fig01.png` | Figure 1: Correct answers under fixed guidance can mask unreliable responses to edits. For the same request, changing its output binding should change the answer, whereas reordering equivalent rules should preserve it. Single-view SFT can fail in either direction: retaining an outdated answer or changing an answer unnecessarily. The figure contrasts these schematic failures with the desired responses. |
| `fig02.png` | Figure 2: Guidance sensitivity after single-view SFT. (a) Candidate-rank transitions at budget 8, with binding edits aligned to original coordinates; diagonal entries indicate unchanged ranks. (b) Response error versus reference uncertainty and label budget on a fixed cohort. Markers locate observed bin rates; colors and contours interpolate their rates in percentile and log-budget coordinates. |
| `fig03.png` | Figure 3: SIGMA training and inference. Training (top): input x and candidates C are shared across guidance g and its edited view g′. (a) Binding swaps require remapped predictions; (b) equivalent reordering requires unchanged predictions. Paired cross-entropy and centered-score alignment update only LoRA parameters (Eq. (5)). Inference (bottom): the frozen adapted model scores all candidates under g_new (top-1 output shown). Animal images are illustrative; model inputs and outputs are text. The diagram's p_g and Π correspond to q_g and P_e in the text. |
| `fig04.png` | Figure 4: Backbone comparison at 5 and 10 shots per intent. Five-seed mean accuracy across three datasets (horizontal panels). Colors identify methods; segments connect Base (open squares) and Comp. (filled circles) accuracies for each backbone and shot budget. Base uses the original guidance; Comp. averages six held-out composition conditions. Within each backbone, methods share labeled subsets, initialization, and candidate scoring. Qwen2.5-7B and Llama-3-8B use Instruct checkpoints; Qwen3-8B uses non-thinking mode. Panels use dataset-specific vertical ranges. |
| `fig05.png` | Figure 5: Annotation–generalization Pareto frontier. Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct; five-seed means over six held-out composition conditions, with ± one standard deviation shaded. Points use N = bm unique labels (m intents; b ∈ {1, 2, 4, 8, 16}) from shared, nested subsets. Lines connect observations; the thick line marks the empirical Pareto frontier across the four methods and tested budgets. Arrows show the smallest tested SIGMA budget exceeding 16-shot LoRA-SFT's mean accuracy (4-shot throughout), excluding fixed development annotations from savings. Table 5 provides full statistics and AULC. |
| `fig06.png` | Figure 6: Margin–residual score diagnostic. Each panel pools 4,926 initially correct trajectories from three 12-candidate diagnostic subsets and k ∈ {2, 4, 8}, so accuracies are conditional on this restricted evaluation. Color shows final correctness smoothed separately across R_mr = 1; white contours show density and dots a deterministic subset without jitter. Above the dashed line (R_mr < 1), Corollary B.4 guarantees a unique correct top-1; insets summarize all records, with "Errors" counting failures in this certified region. |
| `fig07.png` | Figure 7: Directional residual contraction under structured interventions. Each marker is one seed–direction observation, with dataset encoded by color and shape. Negative log-ratios indicate lower residual energy under SIGMA than Paired SFT. Marker area is an affine function of baseline energy, clipped at its pooled 5th and 95th percentiles. Gray contours show pooled density; the dark curve interpolates medians from ten equal-width bins of positive spectral strength, omitting empty bins. The shaded strip marks the operator's null space; all null observations have κ_j = 0. Display jitter separates overlapping non-null points, while all statistics, contours, and trend medians use raw coordinates. Horizontal guides indicate energy ratios of 1/2, 1/4, and 1/8. |
| `fig08.png` | Figure 8: LoRA module knockout effects on guidance composition. Each cell removes one low-rank projection update from SIGMA. Color shows the additional composition-accuracy loss beyond the Base loss, I_guide (pp); hollow circles encode the increase in equivariance error, I_eq, on an absolute TV scale. Circle area is an affine function of I_eq over the observed cell-mean range, with the same mapping in the legend. Cells average three datasets and five seeds equally. Band labels report mean I_guide over layers 0–8, 9–18, and 19–27. Heatmap cells show unsmoothed means; contours are a smoothed visual guide. Circle offsets are confined to their cells and used only for display. |
